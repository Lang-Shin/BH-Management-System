# Halili BH

## Platform

web

## Users

Boarding-house landlords and staff reviewing room occupancy, resident accounts, and payments.

## Product Purpose

Make outstanding balances and recording payments the most prominent daily tasks. Keep room and resident management, transaction history, and reporting easy to reach.

## Capabilities and Constraints

The current React/TypeScript application is a frontend prototype with fictional data stored in one browser. Preserve financial calculations, historical charge ownership, payment review and confirmation, accessible dialogs, and all six routes. Do not imply authentication, synchronization, official receipts, or real payment processing exists.

## Brand Commitments

The user confirmed Halili BH and the green, cream, and red palette. Replace the old layout, typography, cards, and decorative effects completely. Product documentation and UI should focus on the boarding-house product without naming development tools.

## Evidence on Hand

README.md, docs/domain-foundation.md, current domain records, and the running app provide the product facts. Only fictional records are available.

## Accessibility

Preserve keyboard navigation, descriptive labels, visible focus, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
